Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026.

Mail Desk Helper is a private tool, used only by its owner for the owner's own Gmail accounts.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google's gmail.modify scope), it reads the owner's mail, lists and saves drafts, and sends replies the owner writes and approves. It does not delete mail and does not change mail settings.

How the data is used

Mail is read live from Gmail, to show it to the owner and to help the owner research and draft replies. No copy of the mail is kept, apart from attachments the owner opens and the owner's own notes and draft replies. All of these are stored on the owner's own computers.

To help with research and drafts, the text of an email may be sent to an AI model chosen by the owner. That may be a model running on the owner's computer, or a hosted model the owner uses under their own account.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising or used to train models. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time from their Google Account under Security, then Third-party apps with account access.
